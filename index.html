PRIVACY POLICY / 2026

Your personal information deserves protection. LIFE LEVEL is designed around local data storage and user privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

01 INFORMATION COLLECTION

LIFE LEVEL does not require account registration or Google sign-in. The app does not transmit your personal application records to the developer's servers.

02 LOCAL DATA STORAGE

The application may store the following information locally:

  • Gym and workout records
  • Study tasks and achievements
  • Daily schedules and routines
  • Nutrition and water records
  • Personal photos and notes
  • Profile name and profile picture
  • Experience points and level progress

This information is stored within the application's local storage on your device.

03 NO THIRD-PARTY DATA SHARING

LIFE LEVEL does not sell, rent, or share your personal application information with third parties. The developer does not have access to your locally stored app records through the application.

04 OFFLINE & CLOUD STORAGE

LIFE LEVEL does not provide cloud synchronization, online accounts, or cloud backup. Your application records are intended to remain on your device.

05 ADS & TRACKING

LIFE LEVEL does not display advertisements or use third-party advertising or analytics services.

06 PHOTOS & MEDIA

When you select a photo for your profile or Photo Journal, the application accesses your selected image and stores a copy in its local application storage. These photos are not uploaded to developer servers.

07 DATA DELETION

You can delete supported records through the application's available controls. Uninstalling the application generally removes its local application data. Device-level backups, if enabled, are controlled by your device settings.

08 CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

LIFE LEVEL does not knowingly collect children's personal information through online services.

09 POLICY UPDATES

This policy may be updated when application features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
